Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2024

1. Data We Collect

EmotionSense monitors behavioral interactions with websites, including mouse coordinates, click frequency, scroll position, and typing speed.

2. How We Use Data

The data is used solely to predict emotional states for educational support. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

3. Your Consent

By using the EmotionSense extension, you consent to the local monitoring of your browsing behavior during study sessions.

4. Data Storage

Session data is stored locally in your browser's storage and optionally on a local server if you choose to sync your dashboard.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact support@emotionsense.ai
