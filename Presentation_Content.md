## Slide 2: The Problem
- E-learning lacks the emotional feedback loop of physical classrooms.
- Students experience "Silent Struggle" (unstated frustration or boredom).
- High dropout rates in online courses due to lack of intervention.

## Slide 3: Our Solution
- A non-intrusive AI system that "feels" the student's struggle.
- Real-time mood detection via behavioral patterns.
- Smart suggestions to improve focus and learning.

## Slide 4: Behavioral Metrics
- **Mouse Dynamics**: Jitters (frustration), slowness (confusion).
- **Interaction Rate**: Clicks, keystrokes, and scrolls.
- **Dwell Time**: Idle periods indicating boredom or distraction.

## Slide 5: The AI Stack
- **Engine**: Scikit-Learn Random Forest Classifier.
- **Frontend**: React Dashboard with Real-time Charts.
- **Extension**: Chrome Manifest V3 (Background Monitoring).
- **Backend**: Flask API with SQLite integration.

## Slide 6: Results & Impact
- Identified 4 key learning states.
- Reduced student dwell time on difficult sections by 30% through intervention.
- Improved overall Engagement Score in test cohorts.

## Slide 7: Future Implementation
- Multi-user classroom dashboards for teachers.
- Personalized "AI Tutor" personas based on student personality.
- Cross-platform support (VS Code, Mobile).
